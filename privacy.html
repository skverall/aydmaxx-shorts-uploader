This Privacy Policy explains how Aydmaxx Shorts Uploader (the "Tool"), a private internal automation tool, handles data. The Tool is used only by the owners of the Aydan Makhmudov | Agro and Xina Kitchen channels and of the @cardealertracker, @agar.uzb and @aiserial.official Instagram accounts ("the Owners"). It includes the Meta app Aydan Shorts.

1. Data the Tool processes

YouTube. The Tool accesses YouTube data through YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3) for the authorized channels only: the channel identifier, videos that are uploaded through the Tool, custom thumbnails, subtitles (captions) and scheduled publish times. The Tool also stores OAuth credentials (an access/refresh token) locally on the Owner's computer in the macOS Keychain.

Instagram. Through the Instagram API, for the Owners' own Instagram professional accounts (@aydan_makhmudov, @xinakitchen, @cardealertracker, @agar.uzb, @aiserial.official) only: the account identifier and username, the Owners' own posts (Reels, captions, covers), public comments that other people leave on the Owners' own posts (the comment text, the commenter's Instagram username, the comment time and identifier) and, for @aydan_makhmudov and @xinakitchen, direct messages that people send to these accounts (the message text, the sender's Instagram username and identifier, the message time). When the Owners ask, the Tool also looks up, through Instagram's Business Discovery, the public statistics of public professional (business or creator) accounts that commented on or wrote to the Owners' accounts: username, name, follower count, number of posts, bio and website. The Tool does not access follower lists, profile photos or any other data about commenters or senders.

Telegram. The Tool posts the Owners' own videos to the Owners' Telegram channels and sends private notifications to the Owner's own Telegram chat through the Telegram Bot API.

2. How data is used

  • To upload the Owners' own videos to the Owners' own YouTube channels, Instagram accounts and Telegram channels, and to manage those videos.
  • To help the Owners reply to comments and direct messages. When a new comment appears on an Owner's post or a new direct message arrives, the Tool sends the comment or message text, the person's username, the post caption and the Owners' own notes about the video to Anthropic's Claude model to draft a reply (if Claude is unavailable, to Google's Gemini model as a backup; for @aiserial.official there is no backup and Google's Gemini is not used), then shows the text and the draft to the Owner in Telegram. Since October 28, 2026, on @aydan_makhmudov and @xinakitchen, replies to ordinary comments (thanks, short stories and questions answered in the video) are posted automatically, some time after the comment and at a limited pace; emoji-only comments and comments addressed to other people are left without a reply. Comments about purchases or prices, complaints, health, questions about AI, unclear comments and all direct messages are shown to the Owner, and a reply to them is posted or sent only after the Owner approves it. On @cardealertracker, @agar.uzb and @aiserial.official every reply is posted only after the Owner approves it. The Owner can edit, rewrite or skip a reply, hide an obvious spam comment, pause automatic replies at any time, and sees a daily summary with an option to remove any automatic reply.
  • To help the Owners find possible collaboration partners: the Owners see which public professional accounts among the people who commented on or wrote to their accounts are popular in the same field (for example, more than 10,000 followers). The Tool does not contact anyone on its own.

The Tool does not build profiles of people, does not use data for advertising, and makes no automated decisions about anyone. It has no external users.

3. Sharing

No data is sold or rented. Data is transmitted only to the services needed for the functions above: Google/YouTube (YouTube API Services), Meta/Instagram (Instagram API), Anthropic Claude (drafting replies; Anthropic processes this data under its own terms and the Anthropic Privacy Policy), Google Gemini (backup for drafting replies, not used for @aiserial.official; Google processes this data under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy), Telegram (the Owners' channels and the Owner's private notifications) and Cloudflare (R2: temporary hosting of the Owners' own video files and covers so that Instagram can fetch them for publishing; Workers: receiving Instagram's notifications about new direct messages).

4. YouTube API Services and Google policies

This Tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the Tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Instagram and Meta policies

The Tool uses the Instagram Platform in line with the Meta Platform Terms and the Meta Developer Policies. Instagram's handling of data is described in the Instagram Privacy Policy.

6. Data storage and retention

Comment and message data and reply drafts are stored locally on the Owners' computer and are deleted automatically 90 days after they were processed. Instagram's notifications about new direct messages pass through a Cloudflare Worker, where they are kept for at most 7 days until the Tool collects them. Credentials (YouTube OAuth tokens, Instagram and Telegram tokens) are stored locally in the macOS Keychain. Notifications in the Owner's Telegram chat remain under the Owner's control.

7. Deletion and revoking access

If you commented on one of the Owners' posts or sent a message to an Owner's account and want your data deleted from the Tool, email the address below with your Instagram username. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

The Owners can revoke the Tool's access at any time: for YouTube at Google Account → Third-party apps & services (myaccount.google.com/connections), for Instagram in Instagram Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, or by deleting the stored credentials locally. Revoking access stops all further API access.
