Aydmaxx Shorts Uploader is a private, internal automation tool. It is used only by the owners of two YouTube channels — Aydan Makhmudov | Agro and Xina Kitchen — to upload their own original short videos to their own channels through YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3).

The tool uploads video files, sets custom thumbnails, uploads Uzbek subtitles, and schedules each video with a publish time. The same videos are also published to the owners' own Instagram accounts and Telegram channels, and the tool drafts replies to comments on the owners' own Instagram posts, which the owners review and approve one by one. It is not distributed to the public, has no external users, and no user-facing login.
